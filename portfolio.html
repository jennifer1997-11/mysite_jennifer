AI Collaboration, UX Design & MVP Development

Nurtured Bloom

A working pregnancy support MVP with 172 reviewed knowledge records and an 82.8% initial AI test pass rate.

View case study

Background & Objective

Pregnancy information is fragmented and costly to search, making it difficult for expectant mothers to quickly assess its reliability. I planned a pregnancy support platform combining pregnancy management, baby development records, and AI Q&A.

My Role

Product planning and project management, UI/UX design, AI collaboration, and MVP development

Key Execution

Starting from user pain points and feature needs, I developed the information architecture, user flows, interface design, and responsive MVP. I integrated a pregnancy overview, prenatal visit map, consultation notes, pregnancy journal, baby development, and AI pregnancy assistant, and established a medical knowledge base and Q&A testing process.

Outcome & Reflection

Completed a working MVP v1.0 with 172 reviewed pregnancy knowledge records. The AI assistant was tested with 87 questions and reached an initial overall pass rate of 82.8%. This project let me practice the full cycle from requirements and product design to system development and iterative testing, while building hands-on experience in using AI tools to advance digital product development.
